About

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in economics at Télécom Paris, affiliated with the Centre de Recherche en Économie et Statistique (CREST) and the Institut Polytechnique de Paris. I am also an associate researcher at the UMR Développement et Sociétés, and I teach at École Polytechnique and the Institut d’Études du Développement de la Sorbonne (IEDES).

I hold a PhD in economics from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne. My research lies at the intersection of digital and development economics, with a focus on causal impact evaluation and geospatial data analysis. Beyond connectivity, my recent work explores the role of digital platforms and artificial intelligence in shaping labor market dynamics.
