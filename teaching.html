📊 Quantitative Methods & Econometrics

  • Applied Econometrics (École Polytechnique, M1): A course focused on causal inference in social sciences. Topics include RCTs, IVs, RDD, diff-in-diff, event-study, matching, and synthetic control. I lead tutorials based on critical reading.
    Course Materials

  • Applied Econometrics (IEDES, Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne M1): Regression techniques with a focus on nonlinearities, interaction effects, heteroskedasticity, and count/discrete choice models. The course includes theoretical sessions and seminars using R.

  • Collection and analysis of quantitative and qualitative data (UFTAM, M2): Course on research design, survey techniques, and mixed data analysis. Includes a final empirical project using R.

🌍 Development Economics & Policy

  • Development Measurement (IEDES, M1): Covers poverty, inequality, and well-being indicators; measurement issues and aggregation/comparison methods.

  • Development Economics (IEDES, M1): TDs covering core theories, development aid, and public policy.

  • Conferences on policy and project in development (IEDES, M2): A professionalization-oriented course involving guest talks from alumni and practitioners from institutions like the World Bank, AFD, OECD, and NGOs.

📋 Data Science & Replication

  • Data Exploration and Modelling (IEDES, M1): STATA-based analysis of African firm survey data, focusing on gender wage inequality and labor discrimination.

  • Tutored Projects in Development Economics (IEDES, M2): Replication of the paper “This Mine is Mine! How Minerals Fuel Conflicts in Africa” (Berman et al., 2017) using R and STATA. Covers geospatial data, modeling, reproducibility, and empirical presentation skills.
